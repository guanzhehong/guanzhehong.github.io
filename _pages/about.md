I do research in machine learning and artificial intelligence. I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher in [Torr Vision Group](https://torrvision.com/index.html), at the University of Oxford, hosted by Dr. [Philip H.S. Torr](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~phst/). I obtained my BASc in Computer Engineering and Mathematics at the University of Toronto, and PhD at Purdue University, supervised by Dr. [Stanley H. Chan](https://engineering.purdue.edu/ChanGroup/stanleychan.html).

My research focuses on *post-training* and *interpretability*. I am interested in developing principled understanding of the behaviors and learning processes of machine learning models (such as LLMs), and use these insights to improve their generalization and reliability.
